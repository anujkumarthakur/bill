Sabse aasan tarika

Play Protect ka warning aane par settings kholne ki zaroorat nahi hai. Warning dialog me hi “More details” aur phir “Install anyway” par tap karein.

Step-by-step: install kese karein

1
Upar se APK download karein

Download ho jaane par notification ya Downloads folder me file par tap karein.

2
“Allow from this source” par tap karein

Phone puchega to ye permission allow karein (bas jab tak install hota hai).

3
Play Protect ka warning aaye to
App blocked by Play Protect
Google sirf baad ke behtar safety ke liye block kar raha hai.

Darasal is warning me yah dono buttons hote hain:

More details → Install anyway

Doosri baar “More details” dabane par “Install anyway” milega. Isi par tap karein.

4
“Install” par tap karein

App install ho jayega. Installation ke baad phone ko lage to “Done” karke app kholen.

Play Protect ko fully band karna hai (optional)?

Manually kholen: Play Store → profile icon → Play Protect → settings ⚙ → “Scan apps with Play Protect” off. Phir APK dobara install karein.
