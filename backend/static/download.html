✓ Only 3 simple steps

🛡️ Play Protect kese band kare

1
Open Play Protect Settings

Neeche diye gaye button par tap karein. Isse aap directly Play Protect settings page par pahunch jayenge.

2
"Scan apps with Play Protect" band kare

Settings page par "Scan apps with Play Protect" ka toggle off kar dein. (Some phones par isse "Use Play Protect with apps" ya alag naam se bhi dikhaya jaata hai)

3
Wapas aakar APK install karein

Upar download hua APK kholen aur "Install anyway" / "Still install" par tap karein. App install ho jayega.

💡 Tip: Agar button kaam nahi kar raha, to manually kholen:
Play Store → profile icon → Play Protect → settings ⚙️ → "Scan apps with Play Protect" off
